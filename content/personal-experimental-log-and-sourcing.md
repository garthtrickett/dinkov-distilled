## 3. Recipes & Culinary Hacks

* **The Bulgarian Burger:** Mix high-quality ground ruminant meat with gelatin and calcium carbonate (improves calcium-to-phosphorus ratio and lowers prolactin).
* **Butter-Fortified Roast:** When roasting monogastric, higher-PUFA meats (such as chicken or pork), pour off all rendered animal fat. Baste and replace generously with pure butter during the final minutes of cooking.
* **The Raw Carrot Salad:** 1-2 raw carrots grated lengthways, 1 tbsp coconut oil (or extra virgin olive oil), 1 tbsp white vinegar (or apple cider vinegar), and a pinch of salt. Helps bind and excrete endotoxins in the gut.
* **Pre-Bed Glycine Snack:** Warm juice or milk mixed with 1-2 tbsp of gelatin powder and raw honey. Tops off liver glycogen to prevent 4 AM cortisol spikes.
* **Saturated Fat Buffer:** Drizzle a teaspoon of coconut oil or MCT oil over restaurant or processed meals to displace and dilute absorbed PUFAs.
* **Dandruff / Scalp Treatment:** Dissolve 1.5g Salicylic acid and 3g Baking soda in water or Bloo shampoo (glycerol + glucose base). Massage onto scalp and expose to direct sunlight.
