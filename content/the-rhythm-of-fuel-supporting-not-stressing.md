Your eating pattern sends a powerful signal of scarcity or abundance to your body. The goal is to consistently signal abundance.

- **Eat Breakfast, Never Exercise Fasted:** Starting your day with fuel is critical. **Exercising fasted** sends a powerful stress signal, raising cortisol and forcing your body into a catabolic state.

- **Eat Regular Meals:** The primary benefit of frequent meals is the **reduction of endotoxin exposure** from a leaky gut and the prevention of stress-hormone spikes from hypoglycemia.

- **Wait 30 Minutes:** To learn your body's true satiety signals, **wait until thirty minutes after a meal** before deciding if you are still hungry enough to warrant more food.

- **The Golden Rule:** **Eat carbohydrates with your protein/fat meals.** This leverages the "fat burns in the flame of carbohydrate" principle, reduces the need for cortisol-driven gluconeogenesis, and helps keep your thyroid functioning optimally.
