### NAD+/NADH Ratio

As we have established, the NAD⁺/NADH ratio is the ultimate arbiter of your cellular fate. It is the true north on your metabolic compass, the master switch that dictates whether your cells are in a state of efficient energy production or congested, reductive stress.

To recap its critical importance:

- **A High NAD⁺/NADH Ratio** is the hallmark of the oxidized state. It is the "go" signal for efficient glucose oxidation, forward electron flow, and robust CO₂ production. A higher ratio is beneficial, indicating that the cellular power grid is running smoothly.

- **A Low NAD⁺/NADH Ratio** is the signature of the reduced state. It is the "stop" signal of a backed-up system, forcing the cell towards lactate fermentation, fat synthesis, and the production of inflammatory byproducts.
